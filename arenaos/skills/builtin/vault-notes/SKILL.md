---
name: vault-notes
description: Obsidian-style knowledge vault — wikilinks, daily notes, searchable knowledge graph.
---

Keep knowledge in the vault (markdown + [[wikilinks]]):
1. note_write each durable fact, decision, person, or finding — small
   focused notes with clear titles.
2. ALWAYS link related notes with [[wikilinks]] — the graph is the value.
3. note_search before re-creating what exists; merge instead of duplicating.
4. End every task by writing what was learned — future-you reads the vault
   first (note_search beats guessing).
The vault is the operator's memory. Treat it with respect.
